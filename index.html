Find your darts spread, and where you should aim

Throw 50 darts at the centre of the board, type in what each one scored, and this works out how wide your darts land (your spread, σ) and the spot that scores you the most points per dart. From The Maths Of It, episode 1.

Your spread
σ = 30 mm
Best place to aim
 
Count a bounce-out or a miss of the board as 0. 50 darts is good, 100 is better.

How to do the test

  1. Aim every dart at the very centre of the bull.
  2. Write down the score of each dart: 50, 25, a single, a double or a treble.
  3. Paste them here and press the button.

Rule of thumb

Under about 16 mmtreble 20
About 17 to 30 mmtreble 19
Wider than thatlower left, towards the centre

How it works: the board is scored on a 1 mm grid with real ring sizes. Your darts are modelled as a round normal cloud around the aim point, and your spread is the value that makes your 50 scores most likely (the method Ryan Tibshirani describes in Significance). Then every aim point is tested. Real throws often scatter more up and down than side to side, so treat the result as a guide.
